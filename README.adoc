[.lead]
Plush is a small Chrome extension that does three unglamorous jobs in the
softest way it knows how: it turns the volume up to *600%*, carries video from
a sleepy *0.25×* to a breathless *16.00×*, and replaces kbd:[Ctrl+F] with a
real regex-powered search. It wears cream paper and dusty rose, and keeps a
teacup whose steam quickens as you crank the volume — because useful things
deserve nice things too.

This little book is the user manual and the developer reference in one. Make
yourself comfortable; there is tea.

[.center]
✿ ✿ ✿

== 🌸 a warm welcome

* *Volume boost* — real amplification (a Web Audio `GainNode`), not the
  browser's volume slider. Every `<video>` and `<audio>` on an ordinary page,
  including players that arrive late and players hiding in open shadow roots.
* *Playback speed* — `playbackRate` from 0.25× up to Chromium's ceiling of
  16.00×, held there gently but firmly against sites that like to snap it back.
* *Regex find* — a floating search bar with full regular expressions, live
  match counting, keyboard hopping, and highlighting that never touches the
  page's DOM.

Everything is *per-tab*: a 600% / 16× afternoon never leaks into other tabs,
and navigating away tucks everything back in.

[.center]
✿ ✿ ✿

[[requirements]]
== 🫖 what you'll need

Browser:: Any Chromium browser, version 106 or newer — Chrome, Edge, Brave,
Opera, Vivaldi, Arc. Firefox and Safari are not invited (Manifest V3 service
worker; Chromium audio behaviour).
Platform:: Windows, macOS, or Linux.
Privileges:: `storage` (remembers your last find pattern) and content-script
injection on `<all_urls>`. Nothing else — Plush is a light packer.
Disk:: Five small files. No build step, no bundler, no dependencies except
two optional Google Fonts with graceful fallbacks.

✿ ✿ ✿

[[install]]
== 🧶 tucking it in

=== into Chrome

. Make a folder named `plush` holding the five files (<<file-layout>>).
. Open `chrome://extensions`.
. Toggle *Developer mode* (top right).
. Click *Load unpacked* and pick the folder.
. Pin Plush from the puzzle-piece menu, so the teacup is always one click away.

Under *Details*, enabling *Allow access to file URLs* lets boost, speed, and
find visit `file://` pages too.

IMPORTANT: Content scripts are not injected into tabs that were *already open*
when Plush moved in. Reload any tab you'd like it to visit.

[[quickstart]]
== 🍒 three sweet things, quickly

. Open any page with a video.
. Click the teacup; drag *volume* to 600% and *speed* to 16.00.
. Press kbd:[Ctrl+F], type `\d{4}`, press kbd:[Enter] — and hop between every
  four-digit number on the page like a very small detective.

Teacup:: The mascot. Its steam quickens with the volume: one wisp every 3.6 s

[[volume-feature]]
== 🎀 volume — turning it all the way up

=== what "600%" means

100% is untouched audio. Above that, Plush routes each media element through a
Web Audio graph and multiplies sample amplitude — 600% is a gain of 6.0, a
genuine boost rather than nudging the element's `volume` property against its
1.0 maximum.

=== using it

Drag the slider or click a chip; the value pulses and the steam quickens. The
toolbar badge shows the state per tab — `600%` while boosting, `2.5×` when
only speed is set. When both are active, the percentage wins the badge.

=== who it visits

`<video>` and `<audio>` elements, discovered three ways: an initial scan at
page idle, a `MutationObserver` that greets players added later (250 ms
debounce), and a 3-second sweep as a safety net. Discovery descends into open
shadow roots and runs in every frame, so embedded cross-origin players are
covered too.

TIP: Browsers suspend audio contexts until the page is touched. If boost
seems shy right after load, click the page once — Plush listens for gestures
and wakes on its own.

CAUTION: 600% amplifies everything, including peaks the original mix never
expected. Distortion on hot signals is physics, not a bug — and mind your
ears and your speakers.

[[speed-feature]]
== 🌼 speed — the pressed-flower waltz

=== using it

Drag the slider (0.25–16.00) or click a chip. The flower keeps the tempo; the
badge shows the rate while boost rests.

=== the lock

Every discovered element gets a `ratechange` guard: the moment a site's player
strays more than 0.01 from your setting, Plush tucks it back. Players that
arrive *later* inherit the setting automatically. The `1` chip releases the
lock and walks every element home to 1.00.

NOTE: 16.00× is a hard Chromium ceiling — no extension may go beyond.
NOTE: Chromium itself silences audio above roughly 4×. Boost cannot recover
what the browser refuses to mix. Stay ≤4× when you want sound.

[[find-feature]]
== 🔎 find — the little detective

=== opening the bar

._three ways in_
[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|route |how

|in-page (default)
|kbd:[Ctrl+F] — kbd:[Cmd+F] on macOS. The native bar politely steps aside.

|command (rebindable)
|kbd:[Alt+Shift+F]. Rebind at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`.

|from the popup
|Type a pattern in the *find* box, press kbd:[Enter] or the magnifier.
|===

=== reading the bar

A heart, an italic *plush find*, then four buttons: `Aa` (case toggle), prev,
next, close. Below, the input; below that, a status line offering a hint
("type a pattern · enter jumps"), the live count ("3 / 87", or "3 / 2000+"
when truncated), or the regex engine's complaint, verbatim.

=== two ways to write a pattern

Bare form:: The whole string is a regular expression — `\d{4}`. Matching is
case-sensitive by default; the `Aa` button switches to ignore-case.
Literal form:: `/pattern/flags` — `/colou?r/i` — when you'd like to choose
flags yourself.

._the little flags_
[cols="1,3,4",options="header"]
|===
|flag |meaning |notes

|`g` |global |Always forced on — Plush finds *all* the matches.
|`i` |ignore case |Added and removed by the `Aa` toggle.
|`m` |multiline |`^` and `$` meet line boundaries.
|`s` |dotall |`.` makes friends with newlines.
|`u` |unicode |Full code-point matching.
|`v` |unicode sets |Modern set operations.
|`d` |indices |Accepted; harmless — the highlighter doesn't use indices.
|`y` |sticky |Politely declined — sticky scanning would hide matches.
|===

=== hopping between matches

kbd:[Enter] is next, kbd:[Shift+Enter] is previous; both wrap around.
kbd:[Esc] or the close button dismisses the bar and sweeps the highlights
away. The chosen match settles at about 38% from the top of the viewport.

=== what gets searched

All visible text of the top frame, except inside `SCRIPT`, `STYLE`,
`NOSCRIPT`, `TEXTAREA`, `TEMPLATE`, `IFRAME`, `OBJECT`, `EMBED`, `SVG`,
`MATH`, `CANVAS`, `HEAD`, `TITLE`, `OPTION`, and `SELECT` — code, invisible
plumbing, and editable fields are skipped. Shadow DOM and iframes are not
searched; media control works in frames, find does not.

=== highlights that leave no fingerprints

Highlights are absolutely-positioned rectangles in a dedicated overlay layer
pinned to *document coordinates* — they ride along when you scroll and never
enter the page's DOM, so React, Vue, and friends never notice a thing. The
overlay re-measures itself when the DOM changes (~350 ms) or the window
resizes (~200 ms).

=== limits & timing

2000 matched ranges and 6000 painted rectangles per scan (the count reads
`2000+` when truncated). Typing re-runs the search after 160 ms of quiet.

=== 🍪 the pattern cookbook

`\d{4}`:: four-digit runs — years, ports, timestamps
`/colou?r/i`:: colour, color, COLOUR — the literal form
`https?://\S+`:: URLs
`\b(TODO|FIXME)\b`:: work items, hiding
`\b\d{1,3}(\.\d{1,3}){3}\b`:: IPv4 addresses
`[A-Z]{4,}`:: THINGS WRITTEN LIKE THIS

=== the pattern under her pillow

The last pattern is saved to `chrome.storage.local` under `findPattern` when
the bar closes and when launched from the popup; the popup re-fills the box
next time. It never leaves your machine.

[[scope]]
== 🌙 scope, naps & bedtime

* Settings are strictly per-tab. Navigation or reload resets them to 100% and
  1×, and the badge is smoothed away.
* `content.js` runs in *all frames*. Boost and speed are whispered to every
  frame; the hello and the find bar belong to the top frame only.
* The `ratechange` lock and the audio graph attach to elements as they appear,
  for as long as the tab lives.

[[napping]]
Pages where Plush cannot run — `chrome://` and `edge://` pages, the Chrome Web
Store, other extensions' pages, the PDF viewer — show the *napping* card in
the popup instead of controls. A browser boundary, not a mood.

[[dev]]
== 🧵 for the curious — how it's stitched

=== the shape of it

----
┌────────────────┐  boost / speed (all frames)   ┌────────────────────┐
│ popup.html     │ ────────────────────────────▶ │   content.js       │
│ popup.js       │  hello / find (top frame)     │   every frame      │
└────────────────┘ ────────────────────────────▶ └─────────┬──────────┘
                                                           │ drives
                              ┌────────────────────────────┤
                              ▼                            ▼
                  <video>/<audio> + GainNode       regex find overlay
                                                   (top frame only)

┌─────────────┐  {type:'badge', text:'450%'}   ┌────────────────────┐
│ content.js  │ ─────────────────────────────▶ │  background.js     │
│ (top frame) │                                │  (service worker)  │
└─────────────┘                                └────────────────────┘
----

Three surfaces, cleanly separated: the popup never touches the page, the
content script draws nothing beyond its own find bar, and the service worker
only paints badges and relays kbd:[Alt+Shift+F].

._who says what to whom_
[cols="1,2,2,4",options="header"]
|===
|`type` |from ▸ to |payload |what happens

|`hello` |popup ▸ top frame |— |Replies `{ok:true, boost, speed}`. A reply
means the tab is awake; silence means *napping*.
|`boost` |popup ▸ every frame |`value` — percent, 100–600 |Clamped to a gain
of 0–6, applied to all discovered media, badge updated.
|`speed` |popup ▸ every frame |`value` — rate, 0.25–16 |Clamped to
0.0625–16 and locked on. A value of `1` releases and resets.
|`find` |popup ▸ top frame |`pattern` — regex string |Opens the bar
pre-filled; the popup excuses itself.
|`badge` |content ▸ worker |`text` — badge string |Paints the toolbar badge
(#C96F5E). Cleared on navigation.
|===

=== finding the players

`findMedia()` walks a root collecting `video`/`audio` elements and recursing
into open `shadowRoot`s. A `MutationObserver` re-scans added nodes on a
250 ms debounce; a 3-second interval sweeps up stragglers. First touch
installs each element's `ratechange` guard.

=== the audio graph

One graph per element: `MediaElementSource → GainNode → destination`, cached
in a `WeakMap` as `{ctx, gain}` — or `{failed}` if construction threw, so
broken elements aren't retried forever. Routing through the source node is
the whole mechanism: once connected, audio flows only through the graph, and
`gain.value` is your multiplier. Suspended contexts are resumed on the first
gesture (`pointerdown`, `keydown`, `touchstart`). EME-protected (DRM) sources
throw on `createMediaElementSource` and stay unboostable for that page load.

=== the speed lock

Applying speed writes `playbackRate` when it differs by more than 0.01; the
guard rewrites any later deviation. Releasing at `1` resets everything found.

=== find internals

Styles arrive via `CSSStyleSheet` + `adoptedStyleSheets` (immune to page CSP)
with a `<style>` fallback; the Google Fonts link is best-effort and degrades
to system stacks offline or under strict CSP. Scanning is a `TreeWalker` over
text nodes with the skip-list from <<find-feature>>. Each match becomes a
`Range`; its `getClientRects()` become overlay divs at `clientRect + scroll`
inside a layer at `z-index: 2147483646` (pointer-events: none, multiply
blend), the active match tinted and ringed. Re-scans fire on the 160 ms input
debounce, on foreign DOM mutations (350 ms — Plush's own nodes are ignored),
and on resize (200 ms). Input is normalized: the literal `/…/flags` form is
detected, flags de-duplicated, `g` forced, `y` stripped.

|`findPattern` |string |The last regex, reloaded into the popup's box.
|===

[[custom]]
== 🎨 make it yours

=== repaint the card

The palette lives in the `:root` block of `popup.html`: `--card` (paper),
`--blush`/`--blush2` (fills and borders), `--rose`/`--rose-d` (accents),
`--cocoa`/`--cocoa2`/`--faint` (text, muted, faintest). The find bar's colors
sit in the CSS string near the top of `find` in `content.js`:
`.plush-hl` (passive, `rgba(222,124,105,.34)`) and `.plush-hl-on` (active,
`rgba(236,183,94,.75)` plus the ring).

=== give kbd:[Ctrl+F] back to Chrome

Delete this listener near the bottom of `content.js`; the popup and
kbd:[Alt+Shift+F] carry on without it.

[source,js]
----
/* Ctrl/Cmd + F belongs to Plush now — that was the promise */
document.addEventListener('keydown', e => {
  if (IS_TOP && (e.ctrlKey || e.metaKey) && !e.altKey && !e.shiftKey &&
      (e.key === 'f' || e.key === 'F')) {
    e.preventDefault();
    e.stopPropagation();
    find.open();
  }
}, true);
----

=== flip the Aa button's heart

As shipped, matching is case-sensitive and `Aa` switches to *ignore*-case.
For the conventional mapping, one ternary and one initializer:

[source,js]
----
let caseOn = false;            // → true
const fl = caseOn ? 'i' : '';  // → (caseOn ? '' : 'i')
----

=== raise the ceilings

[source,js]
----
// content.js — allow up to 1000%
boost = Math.min(10, Math.max(0, (Number(msg.value) || 100) / 100));

// content.js — bigger find scans
const MAX_MATCHES = 2000, MAX_RECTS = 6000;   // memory is the only cost
----

Also set `max="1000"` on the volume slider in `popup.html`, and update the
track-fill math in `popup.js` from `(v - 100) / 5` to `(v - 100) / 9`.

WARNING: Past ~800%, most material is indistinguishable from a hedge
trimmer. Clipping is guaranteed; treat anything beyond 600% as a curiosity.

=== ideas for little forks

* Persist boost/speed across navigation via `chrome.storage.session`.
* Per-site presets — YouTube always 2×, docs always hushed.
* Iframe-aware find, and a match-replace mode.
* A "midnight linen" dark theme.
* An options page with a pattern history.

[[limitations]]
== 🌧 when things go sideways

DRM / EME media:: Netflix, Disney+, and friends cannot be boosted — the audio
graph cannot attach to protected streams. Speed usually still works.
Above ~4× speed:: Chromium mutes audio. A browser rule, not a setting.
16× ceiling:: Hard-coded in Chromium; unreachable for anyone.
Per-tab reset:: By design. Nothing persists across navigation.
Find and frames:: Matches inside iframes and shadow DOM stay hidden; the bar
itself lives only in the top frame.
Closed shadow roots:: Media inside them is invisible to any content script —
no boost, no speed.
Fixed-position text:: Highlights are document-coordinate; fixed text that
moves without a DOM mutation can drift until the next re-scan. Editing the
pattern forces one.
Fonts:: Offline or CSP-strict pages fall back to system stacks. Cosmetic only.

[[faq]]
== 💌 little questions, answered

Does Plush change settings for the whole browser?:: No — strictly per tab.
Is anything sent to a server?:: No. The only stored data is your last find
pattern, in local storage.
Why did kbd:[Ctrl+F] stop opening Chrome's bar?:: On purpose. Restore it via
<<custom>>, or disable the extension.
Can boost exceed 600%?:: The code clamps at 6.0; raising it is in <<custom>>,
with a warning worth reading first.
Does speed work on YouTube? Netflix?:: Yes, and usually — but never boost on
DRM sites.
Can it search *and* replace?:: Find only.
Where is my pattern kept?:: `chrome.storage.local`, key `findPattern`, on
your machine only.
Is there a mobile version?:: No — desktop Chromium only.

[[privacy]]
== 🔒 privacy, softly

* Plush's own code makes no network requests and keeps no analytics.
* The single external fetch is a Google Fonts stylesheet (Fraunces and
  Quicksand), loaded by the popup and, best-effort, by the find bar. Offline
  or CSP-strict, it is skipped. Privacy maximalists may delete the two font
  links and lose nothing functional.
* Page content is read only to locate media and find matches. Nothing is
  logged, transmitted, or stored beyond `findPattern`.

[[uninstall]]
== 👋 goodbye for now

`chrome://extensions` → Plush → *Remove*. The one stored pattern is deleted
with it, and any tab left at 600% / 16× settles down on its next navigation.
Thank you for having Plush over. ♡
